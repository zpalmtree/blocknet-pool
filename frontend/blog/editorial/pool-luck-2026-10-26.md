# Pool luck article evidence

Checked October 26, 2026 (EDT). Source article: `mining-pool-luck-explained`.

## Implementation sources

- `apps/blocknet-pool-api/src/api.rs`: completed round effort is accepted share
  work / block difficulty × 100; current effort uses current difficulty.
- The same API derives expected seconds as ROUND_TARGET_SECONDS (300) × network
  hashrate / pool hashrate. Elapsed-time percentage and share-work effort have
  different inputs and must not be treated as identical measurements.
- `crates/pool-runtime/src/pgdb.rs`, `avg_effort_pct_up_to`: AVG(effort_pct) for
  non-orphaned blocks with a non-null effort at or below the supplied height.
- `frontend/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx`: Average Luck maps to avg_effort_pct;
  lower values mean less recorded work per block under this convention.
- `frontend/src/pages/LuckPage.tsx`: Block, Effort, Round Time, Status, Found.

## Probability explanation

https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda3667.htm
supports the exponential survival function, constant hazard and mean interval.
Independently evaluated exp(-1), exp(-2), exp(-3) × 100:
36.8%, 13.5%, 5.0% rounded to one decimal. Conditions are stated explicitly:
independent attempts, constant block-finding rate. This is illustrative, not a
forecast or a claim that shares and elapsed time are interchangeable.

Examples with difficulty 10,000 / work 5,000 and 20,000 give 50% and 200%.
Network 100,000 H/s / pool 10,000 H/s gives 3,000 seconds = 50 minutes.
All are arithmetic examples, not measured production benchmarks.
