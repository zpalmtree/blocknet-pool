Sell Blocknet (BNT) on SafeTrade by depositing spendable coins, opening **BNT/USDT**, and placing a sell order. If you mine with seine on bntpool.com, start by checking that the payout has reached your own wallet and is ready to send.

## Where can you sell Blocknet BNT?

<Answer>
Use the [BNT/USDT market on SafeTrade](https://safetrade.com/exchange/BNT-USDT) to sell Blocknet for USDT. Follow the exchange's BNT deposit instructions, wait until the credited coins are available to trade, then submit a sell order at your chosen terms. Confirm the filled amount and USDT proceeds in your trade history.
</Answer>

The exchange's [Blocknet listing announcement](https://safetrade.com/announcements/blocknet-listing) is dated October 28, 2026. Its public market displays the order book, recent trades and order forms. If you are looking to acquire coins instead, use the [Blocknet buying guide](/blog/buy-blocknet-bnt-safetrade/).

## Are your mining rewards ready to send?

For a miner, follow the value through three places: pool accounting, your receiving wallet and your exchange account. Each records a different stage. An estimated reward on bntpool.com is not yet an exchange deposit, and a pool payout transaction does not by itself mean SafeTrade has credited anything.

Open [My Stats](/stats) with the same wallet identity used by your miner. Follow your payout record, then find its incoming transaction in your synchronized wallet. The [PPLNS payout guide](/blog/how-pplns-payouts-work/) explains how rewards become payable. If the pool has sent a payout but you cannot find it, use the [wallet receipt troubleshooting guide](/blog/pool-payout-missing-from-wallet/) before creating a transfer.

Use the wallet's spendable balance when choosing an amount to send. Incoming payments and spendable funds can appear at different times as confirmations accumulate. The [Blocknet transfer-time guide](https://seineminer.com/blog/blocknet-transfer-time/) explains that wallet stage. Keep enough available value for the transaction amount and the charge shown in the wallet's send review.

## Deposit BNT into SafeTrade

Sign in through SafeTrade's official site and complete the access and verification steps required for your account and location. Read its current [terms](https://safetrade.com/terms) and deposit instructions. Select Blocknet BNT and copy the receiving address from your own exchange deposit screen.

Check whether deposits are enabled, the current minimum, required confirmations and any additional deposit information. Use exactly the destination and accompanying fields the exchange provides. If the deposit flow is unavailable, wait until the exchange makes it available before sending. Market trading and deposit availability are separate controls.

In your wallet, prepare a payment to that address. Review the full destination, amount and transaction charge before approving the send. The [desktop wallet guide](https://seineminer.com/blog/blocknet-desktop-wallet/) covers the interface. Save the resulting transaction ID so you can match the wallet send to the exchange deposit record.

Wait for the deposit to become available in your exchange account. A transaction can be broadcast or included in a block before the exchange finishes crediting it. Follow the confirmation requirement shown on the current deposit page rather than assuming the wallet and exchange use the same threshold.

## How do you place a BNT sell order?

Open BNT/USDT and use the sell side of the form. BNT is the asset you are selling; USDT is what you receive. The displayed price is USDT per BNT. Check your available BNT balance before entering the amount, especially if another open order already reserves some of it.

A **limit sell** specifies the lowest price per BNT you are willing to accept and the quantity to sell. For an **illustrative** 100 BNT order at 0.10 USDT per BNT, a complete fill at that price has a trade value of 10 USDT before any applicable trading charge. This example explains the calculation and is not a current market quote.

A limit order can remain open or fill in parts. A **market sell** matches available buy orders; its average execution price depends on the orders it fills. The latest traded price describes a completed trade, while the order book shows the bids available when you review it. Check the sell form and estimated result before confirming your chosen order type.

## Confirm the sale and follow your USDT

Check open orders, filled quantity and trade history after submission. If part of the order remains open, only the filled portion has been sold. Canceling the remainder releases the unsold amount; it does not reverse completed trades. Record the actual executed amount and proceeds rather than the original requested total.

A completed BNT/USDT trade leaves you with USDT in the exchange account. Moving it elsewhere is a separate withdrawal. Select a destination that supports the same asset and network, and review the current minimum, withdrawal charge and receiving instructions. Consult the [SafeTrade fees page](https://safetrade.com/fees) and the final withdrawal form for the applicable figures.

If your goal is money in a bank account, first identify a service that supports converting and withdrawing USDT for your country and chosen currency. Check its account and transfer requirements before sending. The BNT/USDT sale itself ends with USDT, so keep the later conversion and withdrawal records separately.

## Keep mining and trading records connected

Save the pool payout, wallet receipt, wallet send, exchange deposit and completed trades as separate entries. Link them using the relevant transaction IDs and exchange references. This gives you a clear path from earned BNT to credited USDT even when several payouts fund one deposit or an order fills in several trades.

You can continue mining to your own wallet and choose when to transfer spendable BNT. The [pool setup guide](/blog/mine-blocknet-on-bntpool/) explains connecting [seine](https://seineminer.com/) and checking incoming work. Pool reward accounting, wallet transfers and exchange orders each have their own confirmation of completion.
