You can buy Blocknet (BNT) on SafeTrade through its **BNT/USDT** spot market. This guide takes you from the market link to a filled buy order and, if you want to hold the coins yourself, a withdrawal to your Blocknet wallet.

## Where can you buy Blocknet BNT?

<Answer>
Open the [Blocknet BNT/USDT market on SafeTrade](https://safetrade.com/exchange/BNT-USDT). The pair lets you buy BNT with USDT and sell BNT for USDT. Sign in, check the exchange's current account and deposit requirements, fund your available USDT balance, and review a buy order before submitting it.
</Answer>

SafeTrade published its [Blocknet listing announcement](https://safetrade.com/announcements/blocknet-listing) on October 28, 2026. The public market shows an order book, recent trades and Limit, Market and Stop Limit tabs. Use the market itself for the current price and available orders; a price quoted in an article would become outdated.

This article covers buying. If you already hold BNT or have received mining payouts, follow the separate [guide to selling Blocknet](/blog/sell-blocknet-bnt-safetrade/).

## Prepare your account and USDT balance

Create or sign in to your SafeTrade account through the official site. Complete the access and verification steps it presents for your account and location. Review its current [terms](https://safetrade.com/terms) and account requirements before transferring funds. Turn on the account security controls available to you.

Choose USDT in the exchange's deposit flow. Match the asset and network at both ends of the transfer: the sending wallet or exchange must use the network selected for the SafeTrade deposit. Copy the destination from your current deposit screen, and follow any additional information requested there. A familiar ticker alone does not establish that two transfer networks match.

Check the current deposit minimum and required confirmations before sending. Retain the transfer's transaction ID, then wait for SafeTrade to credit the balance as available for trading. A withdrawal marked complete at the sending service and a credited trading balance are separate stages of the transfer.

## What does the BNT/USDT price mean?

The price is the amount of USDT for one BNT. Your intended BNT amount multiplied by the order price gives the trade value before any applicable trading charge. For example, an **illustrative** order for 100 BNT at 0.10 USDT per BNT has a trade value of 10 USDT. These numbers explain the units; they are not a current quote.

The latest trade is one completed transaction. The order book shows offers currently available to match, and the amount offered at one price may be smaller than your planned purchase. Read the buy form's amount and total together so you know whether you are specifying coins to receive or USDT to spend.

## Place and follow a buy order

On BNT/USDT, use the buy side of the trading form. A **limit order** specifies the highest price you are willing to pay per BNT and the quantity you want. Review both fields and the displayed total before submitting. A limit order may fill immediately, partially or remain open while it waits for matching sell orders.

A **market order** buys against available sell orders. Review the exchange's current form and estimate before confirming; the resulting average price depends on the orders it actually matches. Choose the order type deliberately rather than assuming the last traded price applies to your entire purchase.

After submitting, check the exchange's open orders and trade history. An order appearing in the open list does not establish that you have received the full amount. Look for the filled quantity, execution price and resulting BNT balance. If you cancel an unfilled remainder, any portion already filled remains a completed purchase.

## Withdraw BNT to your Blocknet wallet

For self-custody, create or open a wallet using the [Blocknet desktop wallet guide](https://seineminer.com/blog/blocknet-desktop-wallet/). Back up its recovery material privately, then copy its full receiving address. Use that address in SafeTrade's BNT withdrawal flow and confirm that the selected asset is Blocknet.

Review the destination, amount, current withdrawal minimum, charge and any confirmation steps shown by the exchange. The [SafeTrade fees page](https://safetrade.com/fees) provides another place to check its published schedule. Read the final amount shown in the withdrawal form before approving; buying and withdrawing are separate operations.

Keep the withdrawal record and transaction ID when one is available. In your synchronized Blocknet wallet, find the incoming receipt and verify its amount and confirmation state. The [transfer-time guide](https://seineminer.com/blog/blocknet-transfer-time/) explains the difference between a visible receipt and spendable funds. Exchange processing and blockchain confirmation are separate stages, so follow both records.

## After your first purchase

Keep the trade record and any withdrawal record together: one explains how you acquired BNT, while the other explains where you moved it. A withdrawal pending at the exchange should be followed through its status page and support flow; a broadcast payment can also be followed from its transaction ID and receiving wallet.

You can also receive BNT for [goods and services](/blog/accept-blocknet-payments/) or mine it using [seine](https://seineminer.com/). The [fair-launch guide](/blog/blocknet-fair-launch/) explains how mining brings BNT into circulation. Buying existing coins, receiving a payment and earning a mining payout are different ways to acquire the same currency.
