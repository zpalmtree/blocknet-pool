Seine · Exchange deposits

One extra prompt, only when needed.

Personal wallets keep the usual setup. A recognized shared exchange address reveals the deposit-memo prompt.

Implemented, not released. These are terminal-text examples from Seine 4ea5963, not screenshots or a new TUI design. The memo and 10 BNT minimum below are examples.

1. First-run setup

After entering a known exchange address
Enter your Blocknet address or shortname (@name / $name): [your SafeTrade deposit address]
This exchange uses a shared address. A deposit memo is required.
Enter your deposit memo: DEMO001

Setup saves the address and memo together. Your next launch reuses them.

2. Pool connection

Connection log · example pool minimum
Memo accepted. Minimum payout: 10 BNT; larger payouts reduce small exchange deposits.

Seine waits for the pool to acknowledge the exact memo account before accepting work. This confirmation is a log message; there is no persistent exchange or memo badge in the mining screen yet.

Existing setup or an unrecognized exchange

Command-line option · replace both placeholders
./seine --address 'EXCHANGE_ADDRESS' --payout-memo 'YOUR_DEPOSIT_MEMO'

Use the full address and memo from your deposit page. Command-line overrides apply to this run; edit seine-data/seine.config.json to change saved settings.

If the memo is missing or unsupported

A saved known exchange address without a memo stops at startup. It does not open the first-run prompt again:

this shared exchange address requires a memo; use --payout-memo with the memo from your deposit page

If the pool does not confirm memo support:

pool did not acknowledge the payout memo; no shares will be submitted
Pool mining only; solo mining and Seine farm proxies do not support this memo flow. Pool admissions remain disabled until the higher minimum is configured and the updates are deployed.
